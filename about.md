I am Sonyya, and this space is where I keep a record of the things I am learning and building.

I am interested in artificial intelligence, machine learning, software systems, and the mathematics that makes modern computing feel intelligible. I like slow reading, careful implementation, and trying to explain difficult ideas in language that is simple without being shallow.

This site is not a polished portfolio or a performance of expertise. It is a field notebook: a place for technical notes, experiments, and the occasional reflection on how ideas come together.

I write because understanding is a process, not a final state. Some entries will be exploratory, some practical, and some unfinished. That is the point.

If you are here for research notes, implementation experiments, or thoughtful explanations of technical ideas, I hope you find something useful here.
