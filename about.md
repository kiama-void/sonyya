## A learning framework

| Question | What it tests |
|---|---|
| **What?** | Definition and fundamental concept |
| **Why?** | Problem it solves and why it exists |
| **How?** | Working mechanism, architecture, mathematics |
| **When?** | Where and when it is useful |
| **What if?** | Limitations, trade-offs, failure scenarios |
| **What's next?** | Connections to other concepts and future developments |
