Came across [Microduck by Pollen Robotics](https://pollen-robotics.com/microduck/blog/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) and found the idea genuinely exciting.

There's an interesting opportunity in the intersection of affordable hardware, open-source robotics, and multimodal AI.

The Indian toy market could be an interesting starting point. Small, expressive, interactive robots that are not just toys, but programmable companions and platforms for experimentation.

Combining low-cost hardware with vision, voice, autonomous behaviour starting with toys and eventually growing into a broader robotics ecosystem .
