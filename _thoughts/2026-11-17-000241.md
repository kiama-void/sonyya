I feel extremely lucky to be alive in the age of AI.

What moves me most is what it can do for people.

Not everyone has a patient teacher, a good mentor, or someone they feel safe asking for help. So many people never ask at all. They hesitate, bury their doubts, and quietly stay stuck.

Imagine a space where no question is too dumb, no doubt is judged, and every answer can be questioned. A space that helps you think, not tells you what to think.

That kind of access can help break cycles of inherited ignorance and insecurity, one honest question at a time.

I once heard that what one generation can’t figure out, the next gets the chance to. I’ve been handed more knowledge and tools than anyone before me, and I don’t want to waste my turn.

I want to take what I’m learning and make it reachable for many more people, without harming the world along the way.

I want to build technology that gives people the confidence to think for themselves.
